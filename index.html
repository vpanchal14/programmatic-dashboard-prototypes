Concept A
Command Strip
Sticky full-width KPI rail that stays in view while you scroll. Dominant area chart for Spend + Impressions trend, delivery funnel, and campaign efficiency table. Clean teal/cyan SaaS feel — fast morning health-check.
Teal accent Sticky KPIs Area chart Delivery funnel Video funnel

Concept B
Story Flow
Editorial top-to-bottom narrative: Chapter 1 = what happened (KPI cards), Chapter 2 = how it trended (Spend + CTR chart + regions), Chapter 3 = which campaigns (full table), Chapter 4 = AM notes. Warm slate + coral, easy to read on a client call.
Coral accent Chapter layout Bar + line combo Region ranking Callout notes
→ Open Concept B
Concept C
Scorecard Grid
Optimizer-first layout. Violet summary strip across the top, spend + CTR trend charts, then a tiled grid of campaign scorecards each with RAG (red/amber/green) status pills for CTR, eCPM, and CVR. AM can scan 12 campaigns in seconds and know what to fix.
Violet accent RAG status Campaign tiles Optimizer view Dense data
→ Open Concept C
Concept D
Campaign Spotlight
Two-panel master/detail layout. Left sidebar lists all campaigns with RAG status and spend; click any to load a full detail pane — delivery funnel, performance signals, spend + CTR trend charts, and top DMAs. Overview tab shows the aggregate picture. Ideal for client reviews or deep-dives.
